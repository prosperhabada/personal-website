"If you want to change the world, change Education" - Nelson Mandela

I'm an educator and technologist based in Prince Edward Island, Canada. I hold a Master of Education in Educational Leadership (Global Perspective) from the [University of Prince Edward Island](https://www.upei.ca/) and a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from [Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology](https://www.knust.edu.gh/).

Currently, I'm a Learning Manager for the Computer Networking Technology program at [Holland College](https://www.hollandcollege.com/), where I coordinate scheduling, manage instructional resources, and collaborate with program leadership across CNET and Electronic Engineering.

Previously, I led the IT Department at [SOS-Hermann Gmeiner International College](https://www.soshgic.edu.gh/) in Ghana, and I continue to serve as a seasonal IB Examiner for the [International Baccalaureate Organization](https://www.ibo.org/). I also built web applications at [Tema Oil Refinery](https://torghana.gov.gh/) and [Vivisoft Ghana](https://www.vivisoftghana.com).

I still tinker with Arduinos and Raspberry Pis when I have the time, and I love the feeling of floating on water... j'adore la natation.
